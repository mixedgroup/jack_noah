Welcome







Dear clinician, welcome to the virtual patient pilot clinical training!

We are very grateful for your participation, which will help us develop an AI-based virtual patient training tool in clinical interviewing and suicide risk assessment.
In this pilot training, we will ask you to conduct an initial clinical interview with the patient in order to assess their main problem and their risk level. We encourage you to apply what you learned in class.

After the interaction, we will ask you to complete a 15 min survey collecting your feedback about your interaction with the virtual patient and we will also provide you with descriptive feedback (a summary) on your interaction with the virtual patient, which will focus on the interview process and your approach.

On the interaction screen, you will see your virtual patient. On the top left of the screen, you will be provided with basic information about the patient.

On the right, you will see a dialogue window labeled “Chat History”.

The virtual patient can talk, but he cannot understand spoken language. Therefore, please type your questions in the dialogue window. Every time you type a question, your virtual patient will respond orally.

On the bottom left of the screen, you will see an End Interview button. Please click the button when you finish the interaction with the virtual patient.
A few tips to make your interview easier:

  1. Remember that this is a virtual patient. Sometimes, he may not understand your question. Additionally, the virtual patient cannot understand blended statements with several questions. Please ask one question at a time.

    For example, the virtual patient struggles to understand blended statements with an empathic statement AND a question. Please separate them. In addition, instead of saying: “I am sorry to hear that, please tell me more about your mood”. Say: “I am sorry to hear that”. The virtual patient will answer. Then, after the virtual patient has answered, you may ask: “Please tell me more about your mood”.

  2. The virtual patient needs specific questions. Instead of asking "Why is that?" or "Why?", please provide more information. You may ask: "why are you upset?" or "why do you hate such and such...?"

  3. If the virtual patient provides an answer that does not make sense to you, please ignore it and continue your interview. This may happen a few times, and will help us improve the artificial intelligence algorithm that is powering the virtual patient.



Before you start the interaction with the virtual patient, please complete the Before Interaction Survey. Press the button to begin.
